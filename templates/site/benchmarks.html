{{ icon('benchmark') }}

What we benchmark

Cash CTC at P25, P50 and P75 for a role, level, city and funding stage, plus an ESOP grant range. Company health never moves the market range; it moves where you aim inside it.

{{ icon('cohort') }}

Peer cohort

The exact cohort is shown, e.g. "Software Engineer · Senior · Tier 1 SaaS Hub · Series A". If it's too small it widens step by step, and the widening is labelled.

{{ icon('runway') }}

Observations and recency

Every benchmark shows the observation count, the number of companies behind it, and the data period. Old data lowers confidence.

{{ icon('confidence') }}

Confidence changes the output

High: full range and a target. Medium: a widened range. Low: a broad band, no target, and an acknowledgment. Insufficient: no recommendation at all.

{{ icon('outcome') }}

Offer-outcome telemetry

Payroll data only sees people who accepted. CompIntel also records counters, declines and decline reasons, so the range reflects where offers actually clear.

{{ icon('consent') }}

Privacy thresholds

A real-data range appears only when a cohort has at least 10 accepted offers from at least 3 companies. Companies are hashed; candidates are never named.
