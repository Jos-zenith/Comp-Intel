What candidates actually ask about equity

{{ icon('esop') }}

Percentage or option count?

A percentage only means something against fully diluted shares. Show both, and what the percentage becomes after the next rounds.

{{ icon('cash') }}

Strike price and FMV

The gap between strike and FMV is the value today; the strike times the option count is the cash needed to exercise.

{{ icon('runway') }}

Vesting and cliff

Most Indian schemes vest over four years after a one-year cliff, and the rules generally require at least a year between grant and vesting.

{{ icon('cohort') }}

Dilution

Future rounds shrink every holder's percentage. A scenario that ignores dilution overstates the outcome.

{{ icon('compare') }}

Three scenarios, never one number

Conservative, Base and Upside side by side, so nobody presents an illiquid option as a certain amount.

A candidate-safe explanation
