Why structure matters before the offer

Two offers with the same CTC can carry different PF and gratuity liabilities and very different take-home. Fixing that after the letter is signed is expensive.

{{ icon('cash') }}

Cash CTC, employer cost, take-home

Cash CTC is what you budget. Employer cost includes your PF and gratuity accrual. Take-home is what the candidate compares against their current pay.

{{ icon('audit') }}

Statutory wage under the Code on Wages

Wages are Basic + DA. If excluded allowances exceed 50% of remuneration, the excess is added back for PF and gratuity, which can differ from the Basic line.
