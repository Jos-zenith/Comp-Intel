Questions

What does "contributor" mean?

You share anonymised offer outcomes (role, level, city tier, offer and decision, no names) and get private benchmarks for your open roles in return. Your company's financials are never shared.

Are today's benchmarks real?

Not yet. The demo and this site run on a synthetic, calibrated dataset, and say so. Pilot workspaces benchmark only from real, consented offers once a cohort clears our privacy thresholds.

Can we stop sharing later?

Yes. Switch sharing off and your future outcomes stay private. Design partners can withdraw imported data, and it stops feeding benchmarks immediately.

Do you do payroll or statutory filings?

No. CompIntel is decision support before the offer. CTC and Wage Code checks are not legal or payroll-audit advice.
