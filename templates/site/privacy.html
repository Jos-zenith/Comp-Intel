Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Draft - pending legal review. This plain-language summary describes how the current software handles data. It is not yet a reviewed legal policy and must be replaced by counsel-approved text before launch.

What this website collects

The sample benchmark, CTC calculator and ESOP calculator store nothing you type. We record that a calculator was used (for example the role, level, city and stage of a sample benchmark) without your IP address, browser details or any free text. If you apply for the pilot, we store the details you submit - name, work email, company, role, company size, hiring volume, cities and notes - to contact you about the pilot.

What a workspace stores

Company profile and metrics, internal bands, requisitions, offers, approvals and outcomes, all private to the workspace. Candidates are identified by a pseudonymous ID; a candidate's name is optional and is removed from closed offers after the workspace's retention period.

What is shared, and when

Only with the workspace's consent, and only anonymised offer outcomes: role, level, city tier, funding stage, company-size / ARR / NDR bands, offer amounts, ESOP grant, outcome and decline reason. Company identity is replaced by a salted hash. Company financials, names and internal notes are never shared. A benchmark built from shared data appears only when enough companies contribute.

Your choices

Workspaces can switch data sharing off at any time. Design partners can withdraw imported data. To ask about access, correction or deletion, use the pilot form and say so in the notes.

To be completed by counsel: data controller details, legal basis under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, grievance officer, retention schedule, processors and cross-border transfers.
